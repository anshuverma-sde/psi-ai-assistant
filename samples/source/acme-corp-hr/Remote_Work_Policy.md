# Acme Corp — Remote Work Policy

Human Resources · Policy HR-09 · Effective March 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp supports flexible working where the role allows it. This policy defines who may work remotely, how much, and what support the company provides.

## 2. Eligibility for Remote Work

An employee is eligible to work remotely when ALL of the following conditions are met:

- The employee has completed at least 12 months of continuous service with Acme Corp.
- The employee's manager has approved the remote work arrangement in the HR Portal.
- The employee's role is not designated as on-site only. On-site only roles include warehouse, reception and lab positions.

\pagebreak

## 3. Work Arrangements

| Arrangement | Remote days per week | Approval needed |
| Hybrid | 1 to 3 days | Manager |
| Fully remote | 5 days | Manager and Vice President |

Employees on a hybrid arrangement must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays for team days.

## 4. Remote Work Stipend

Eligible employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week receive a remote work stipend of $50 per month to cover internet and utility costs. The stipend is paid with the monthly salary and stops automatically if the remote work arrangement ends.

\pagebreak

## 5. Equipment

Acme Corp provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset to every remote-eligible employee. A one-time home office allowance of $300 is available for a desk or chair, claimed through ExpensePoint within the first 6 months of the arrangement.

## 6. Working From Another Country

Employees may work remotely from another country for at most 30 days per calendar year. This requires approval from HR at least 15 working days in advance, because of tax and insurance rules.

## 7. How to Request Remote Work

1. Discuss the arrangement with your manager.
2. Submit the Remote Work Request in the HR Portal, selecting hybrid or fully remote.
3. Your manager approves or declines within 5 working days; fully remote requests are then forwarded to the Vice President.
