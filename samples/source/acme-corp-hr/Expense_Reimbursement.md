# Acme Corp — Expense Reimbursement Policy

Finance Department · Policy FIN-02 · Effective January 1, 2026

## 1. General Principles

Acme Corp reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees incur while performing their job. Expenses must be necessary, supported by itemised receipts, and submitted on time. Personal expenses, fines, alcohol and entertainment that is not approved in advance are never reimbursed.

## 2. How to Submit an Expense Claim

1. Log in to ExpensePoint, Acme's expense system.
2. Create a new claim and add each expense with its date, category and amount.
3. Upload a photo or scan of every itemised receipt.
4. Submit the claim within 30 days of the expense date. Claims submitted later than 30 days are rejected.
5. Your manager approves the claim, and Finance pays approved claims with the next monthly salary.

\pagebreak

## 3. Spending Limits

| Category | Domestic travel | International travel |
| Meals (per day) | $60 | $90 |
| Lodging (per night) | $200 | $300 |
| Local transport (per day) | $40 | $60 |

Expenses above these limits are only reimbursed when approved in writing by a Director before the trip.

## 4. Mileage

Employees who use their personal car for business trips are reimbursed $0.60 per kilometre, but only for trips longer than 50 km (one way). Commuting between home and the usual office is never reimbursed.

\pagebreak

## 5. Approval Thresholds

- Claims up to $500 require approval from the employee's manager.
- Claims above $500 require approval from both the manager and the department Director.
- Claims above $5,000 additionally require approval from the Chief Financial Officer.

## 6. Corporate Cards

Employees who travel more than 4 times per year may request a corporate card. Corporate card statements must be reconciled in ExpensePoint within 15 days of the statement date.
