# Acme Corp — Leave Policy

Human Resources · Policy HR-04 · Effective January 1, 2026

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted hours.

## 2. Annual Leave

Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues at 1.67 days per month of service. Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused days into the next calendar year; any other unused days are forfeited on December 31. Annual leave requests must be submitted in the HR Portal at least 10 working days in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

\pagebreak

## 3. Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not carry over to the next year. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, the employee must provide a doctor's note within 3 days of returning to work. Employees must inform their manager before the start of the working day when they are sick.

## 4. Casual Leave

Employees receive 6 days of paid casual leave per calendar year for personal matters. Casual leave can be taken for at most 2 consecutive days at a time and must be requested at least 1 working day in advance. Casual leave cannot be carried over and cannot be combined with annual leave in the same request.

## 5. Comparison of Leave Types

| Leave type | Days per year | Carry over | Notice required |
| Annual leave | 20 | Up to 5 days | 10 working days |
| Sick leave | 10 | No | Inform manager same day |
| Casual leave | 6 | No | 1 working day |

\pagebreak

## 6. Parental Leave

Employees who have completed at least 6 months of continuous service are eligible for paid parental leave. The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of paid leave and the secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## 7. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons. Unpaid leave requires approval from both the manager and HR, and is only available once all annual leave has been used.

## 8. Requesting Leave

1. Open the HR Portal and select "Leave Request".
2. Choose the leave type and dates.
3. Attach supporting documents if required (for example a doctor's note).
4. Submit the request; your manager is notified automatically and responds within 3 working days.
