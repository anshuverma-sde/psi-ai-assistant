# Northbridge University — Academic Policies

Office of the Registrar · Academic Year 2026–2027 · Document REG-200

## 1. Academic Calendar

The fall semester starts on September 7 and ends on December 18. The spring semester starts on January 18 and ends on May 14. Final examinations take place in the last two weeks of each semester.

## 2. Credit Load

A full-time student takes at least 12 credits per semester. A student taking fewer than 12 credits is a part-time student. The maximum load is 20 credits per semester; taking more than 20 credits requires approval from the student's academic advisor. A bachelor's degree requires 120 credits.

## 3. Grading Scale

| Letter grade | Grade points |
| A | 4.0 |
| B | 3.0 |
| C | 2.0 |
| D | 1.0 |
| F | 0.0 |

\pagebreak

## 4. Attendance Policy

Students are expected to attend all classes. A student may miss up to 3 unexcused classes per course per semester. Each additional unexcused absence lowers the final course grade by one third of a letter grade (for example from B to B-). Absences for documented illness or family emergencies are excused when the student submits the Absence Form within 7 days.

## 5. Dean's List

A student is placed on the Dean's List for a semester when they earn a semester GPA of at least 3.7 while taking at least 15 credits, with no grade below B in that semester.

## 6. Academic Probation

A student whose cumulative GPA falls below 2.0 is placed on academic probation. Students on probation may take at most 13 credits per semester and must meet with their advisor every month. A student who remains on probation for two consecutive semesters may be dismissed.

\pagebreak

## 7. Course Withdrawal

A student may withdraw from a course until the end of week 8 of the semester. Withdrawals appear as "W" on the transcript and do not affect the GPA. After week 8, withdrawal is only possible for documented medical reasons.

## 8. Grade Appeals

To appeal a final grade:

1. Contact the course instructor within 10 days after grades are published.
2. If the issue is not resolved, submit the Grade Appeal Form to the Department Head within 20 days after grades are published.
3. The Department Head decides within 15 working days. A further appeal can be made to the Dean, whose decision is final.

## 9. Academic Integrity

Plagiarism, cheating and falsifying data are serious offences. A first offence results in a zero for the assignment. A second offence results in failing the course and a record of academic misconduct. A third offence may result in dismissal from the university.
