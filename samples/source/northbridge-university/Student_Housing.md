# Northbridge University — Student Housing Guide

Residence Life Office · Academic Year 2026–2027 · Document RES-300

## 1. Who Can Live On Campus

All first-year students are guaranteed a place in on-campus housing if they submit the Housing Application by June 1. Returning students are allocated rooms by lottery, subject to availability. Students must be at least 18 years old to sign the housing contract themselves; students under 18 need a parent or guardian to co-sign.

## 2. Room Types and Prices

| Room type | Occupants | Price per semester |
| Single room | 1 | $3,600 |
| Shared double room | 2 | $2,400 |
| Shared triple room | 3 | $1,900 |

Single rooms are allocated first to students with documented medical needs. Prices include electricity, water, heating and Wi-Fi.

\pagebreak

## 3. House Rules

- Quiet hours are from 10 pm to 7 am every day.
- Guests may stay overnight for at most 2 nights per week and must be registered at the front desk.
- Pets are not allowed in student housing. Registered service animals are permitted.
- Smoking and candles are not allowed anywhere in the residence halls.

## 4. Maintenance Requests

To report a maintenance problem, submit a request through the Housing Portal under "Maintenance". Urgent issues (water leaks, no heating, broken locks) must also be reported by phone to the Residence Life Office on 555-0142, which is staffed 24 hours a day. Non-urgent requests are handled within 3 working days.

\pagebreak

## 5. Cancelling a Housing Contract

1. Submit the Housing Cancellation Form through the Housing Portal.
2. Cancellations received before August 1 are free of charge.
3. Cancellations received after August 1 are charged a $500 cancellation fee.
4. After the semester starts, students remain responsible for the full semester's rent unless they withdraw from the university.

## 6. Move-In and Move-Out

Move-in for the fall semester is on September 4 and 5. Students must move out within 24 hours of their last final examination. Rooms are inspected at move-out and repair costs for damage are charged to the student's account.
